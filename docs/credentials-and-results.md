## 2. Administrative Role Provisioning
In this "Zero-Trust" environment, a user becomes an administrator only when two conditions are met:
1.  Their document in the `/users/{userId}` collection has the field `role: "admin"`.
2.  A document exists in the restricted `/admin_roles/{userId}` collection (where `{userId}` is their unique Firebase UID).
